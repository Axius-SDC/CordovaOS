Today

This question normally starts an integration project. Four systems, each holding part of the answer, each with its own idea of what identifies a person.

Nothing here was integrated. The four domains use the same published component for the national identifier, so the join is the identifier itself. There is no mapping table to maintain and no reconciliation step to schedule, which is why the answer takes {{ q.elapsed }} seconds rather than a quarter.

What this does not show. The question a minister would rather ask, which businesses employ exposed people and what trade is at risk, cannot be answered here: employment records carry the employer as free text rather than as a business identifier, so the chain breaks between Employment and Business Registry. That is a modelling gap in the demonstration data, and naming it is more useful than staging around it.
